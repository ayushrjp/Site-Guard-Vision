Abstract

Preliminary · Page 05

SiteGuardVision is an image-only Flask application for reviewing construction-site photographs.

Separate YOLO-based checkpoints detect PPE items and people; geometry-based association relates detected hard hats and vests to worker boxes.

The application reports Potential Violation, No Violation Detected, or Insufficient Evidence rather than asserting legal compliance.

SQLite persists analysis history and settings.

An optional document-grounded assistant combines PDF/TXT/Markdown ingestion, ChromaDB retrieval and local Ollama generation.

Custom evaluation on 90 test images reported PPE-only macro precision 0.842, recall 0.734, mAP50 0.847 and mAP50–95 0.521.

The full application reported 21 potential violations, 5 no-violation detections and 64 insufficient-evidence outcomes; annotation incompleteness and person-count error constrain interpretation.

Ollama/RAG operation was not live-verified in the captured environment.

1 Introduction

Chapter · Page 08

Construction sites contain changing work fronts, temporary access routes, variable lighting and workers who may be partially occluded.

Personal protective equipment (PPE) such as hard hats and high-visibility vests reduces exposure to certain hazards when correctly selected and worn.

Manual observation can be intermittent, while fixed cameras and computer-vision models offer a way to flag image evidence for human review.

A detector does not understand the complete work context and must not be treated as a safety authority.

1 Introduction — Scope and Context

Chapter · Page 09

The engineering problem addressed here is deliberately narrower than continuous surveillance: a user submits one photograph, the application detects people and PPE, associates evidence spatially, and presents a cautious assessment with an annotated image.

It is an image-review decision-support prototype, not a live video monitor, identity system, autonomous enforcement mechanism or substitute for site inspection.

2 Problem Statement and Objectives

Chapter · Page 12

The core problem is to make PPE evidence in a construction photograph easier to review without converting uncertain detections into categorical compliance claims.

False negatives can hide a missing item; false positives can create unnecessary escalation.

A third outcome—insufficient evidence—makes uncertainty visible when detected equipment cannot be confidently associated or workers are not adequately observed.

Object detection methods predict object categories and bounding boxes in one or more image passes.

Conventional PPE inspection includes direct supervisor observation, checklists, access-control procedures and camera-assisted review.

Human inspection provides context that image models lack, but coverage depends on time and vantage point.

3 Literature Survey and Existing Systems — Implementation Detail

Chapter · Page 17

A detector typically produces class, confidence and box coordinates.

Non-maximum suppression (NMS) reduces overlapping predictions within inference, while subsequent association logic assigns PPE evidence to people.

These are distinct steps: NMS does not solve worker-to-equipment assignment and box overlap alone does not establish that an item is worn by a particular worker.

3 Literature Survey and Existing Systems — Evidence and Interpretation

Chapter · Page 18

Precision is TP/(TP+FP) and recall is TP/(TP+FN).

Average precision integrates precision over recall; mAP averages AP across classes and, in mAP50–95, IoU thresholds from 0.50 to 0.95.

Metric definitions depend on matching, class mapping, confidence filtering and averaging.

The included custom scripts state their greedy IoU matching and 101-point interpolation; their scores must not be described as official Ultralytics validation.

9 Worker-to-PPE Association and Safety Assessment

Chapter · Page 47

For each detected person box, the engine derives smaller head and torso regions from relative coordinates.

A hard-hat candidate is considered against the head region; vest evidence is considered against the torso region.

Geometric overlap and centre-point inclusion provide complementary cues when predicted object boxes have different sizes.

9 Worker-to-PPE Association and Safety Assessment — Scope and Context

Chapter · Page 48

Intersection over Area (IoA) divides the intersection area by the area of a selected box, unlike IoU, which divides by the union.

IoA is useful when one object box is substantially contained within a worker subregion.

The implementation uses configured geometric thresholds; this is a heuristic association, not pose estimation or a learned person-item relation.

SiteGuardVision · [Institution] · 48

9 Worker-to-PPE Association and Safety Assessment — Implementation Detail

Chapter · Page 49

Matching is constrained to avoid assigning one PPE detection to multiple workers.

Duplicate detections are suppressed and detections that cannot be assigned remain unassigned evidence.

In crowded scenes, overlapping worker regions, truncated bodies and perspective can still yield ambiguous or incorrect assignments.

14 Conclusion

Chapter · Page 66

SiteGuardVision implements a cautious image-based PPE review workflow combining separate person and PPE detectors, geometry-based assignment, a three-state assessment, Flask/Jinja dashboard, SQLite history and an optional local document-grounded assistant.

Saved custom evaluations show promising PPE precision alongside missed detections, person-count errors and incomplete annotation concerns.

The 90-image application evaluation’s 64 insufficient-evidence outcomes underscore the system’s conservative design and current limits.

The prototype is decision support only; it does not certify compliance.

Before operational use, annotation quality, model performance, RAG live behavior, privacy and deployment controls require further independent verification.
